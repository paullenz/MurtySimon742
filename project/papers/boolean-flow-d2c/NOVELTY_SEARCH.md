# Novelty search — Boolean-flow D2C paper

**Status:** preliminary only. No novelty statement authorised.

## Confirmed neighbouring literature from preliminary search

A quick search on 17 September 2026 located the established D2C literature including:

- G. Fan, *On diameter 2-critical graphs* (Discrete Mathematics, 1987);
- later Murty–Simon/total-domination work and special-class characterisations;
- the strengthening/second-extremal programme in the 2019-era literature;
- J. Radosavljević, *Diameter-2-critical graphs with at most 13 nodes* (2023), reporting a unique small counterexample among the tested orders;
- Radosavljević–Stanić–Živković, *Primitive diameter 2-critical graphs* (2024), containing structural results and new constructions;
- the 2025 Discrete Applied Mathematics paper characterising sufficiently large `C5`-free D2C graphs above the `floor((n-1)^2/4)+1` threshold.

The preliminary search did not reveal a result described explicitly as a Boolean-coordinate flow/factorial path theorem for the exact zero-residual root boundary. Nor did it clear the hypercube-face family as new. A serious construction-level comparison is required.

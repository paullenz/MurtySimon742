# Reviewer-paper preservation policy

Effective 9 September 2026.

For this project, a theorem-level candidate result is not considered **review ready** merely because a proof Markdown file or computational certificate exists.

Before a frozen theorem-level candidate becomes the canonical public result, preserve:

1. a standalone reviewer manuscript containing the complete current proof;
2. a verification/replay companion when computation, formal checking or substantial audit material is involved;
3. explicit provenance and status language distinguishing internal checks, same-assistant reimplementations and genuinely independent external review;
4. a manifest or equivalent integrity record for the reviewer package;
5. a link from `releases/REVIEW_READY_INDEX.md`.

Failed, falsified and superseded approaches remain preserved but are not promoted into reviewer papers unless they are independently useful mathematical results. Work in progress should stay labelled as research until a theorem-level statement is frozen.

This policy does not promote the mathematical status of any result. Independent specialist review and external computational reproduction remain separate obligations where applicable.

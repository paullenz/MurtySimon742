# Large-surplus live-strip collision screen

23 September 2026. Status: **FINITE INTERNAL NEGATIVE EVIDENCE; NO UNIVERSAL CLAIM**.

After the live-strip positive-demand collision negative control was found, the search was retargeted to the only regime relevant to a strict residual counterexample: large total demand S.

A fresh independently generated and edge-deletion-certified batch covering n=17..32 and seeds 0..99 contained 1,600 D2C graphs, 144 live-strip maximum-degree root states, 1,037 co-selected F-edge candidate occurrences, and 45 crossed-supplement collision images. Every G[B]-edge in the inspected root states had an endpoint-label certificate.

Only two collision images joined two graph-eligible labels (2s_i>h_i at both endpoints). Exact certificate-choice MILP with the collision certificates forced found:

- one jointly positive-demand collision feasible: the already preserved n=23, seed 47, root 7, labels {1,4}, supplements {0,20};
- its maximum total demand over **all** legal completions is S=3;
- the other ELIG--ELIG collision is jointly positive-demand infeasible.

Therefore this entire finite batch contains **no live-strip positive-demand collision compatible with S>=15**. This does not prove an S>=15 exclusion theorem, but it materially changes the attack target: the universal positivity exclusion is false, whereas a large-surplus version survives the present actual-graph screen.

Next theorem target: prove or refute that an ELIG--ELIG crossed-supplement collision in the live strip forces total demand S<=14, or more generally derive a quantitative collision charge strong enough that any strict residual counterexample (which already requires S>=15 internally) cannot realize enough repeated supplement-pair capacity.

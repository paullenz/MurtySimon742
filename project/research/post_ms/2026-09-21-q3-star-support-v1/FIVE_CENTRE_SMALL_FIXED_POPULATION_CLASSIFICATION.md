# Exact small fixed-population classification at five centres

21 September 2026.  Exact SAT enumeration; scope is one physical copy of
each selected code on centre support `{0,1,2,4,7}`.

Among all subsets of the eight nonstar antipodal-transversal codes, at least
seven are required.  At seven, the unique feasible code subset is

    C00,C01,C10,C11,C20,C21,P1.

There are exactly three labelled A-graphs.  All have the P1 vertex joined to
the six coordinate vertices, the edge S0--S7, and a two-edge path on
`{S1,S2,S4}`.  The three choices of the path centre are related by coordinate
permutations, so this is one graph up to the support symmetry.  Every model
has `n=21,m=77`.

With all eight halfcodes present, there are exactly seven labelled A-graphs:
six have ten A-edges and one has eleven.  The unique labelled maximum is the
`n=22,m=83` star-hub base: the P1 hub meets all coordinates, P0--P1 is present,
and S7 is joined to each of S0,S1,S2,S4.

There is a second 83-edge route in the complete one-extra slice from the
order-21 minimum population: omit P0 and add a second S7 instead.  Exact
enumeration of all 13 possible added types shows that only added P0 and added
S7 attain 83 edges.  Coordinate or S1/S2/S4 additions attain at most 82,
another P1 attains 81, and another S0 is infeasible.  Thus order 22 already
has two distinct population mechanisms at the top of this near-minimal slice;
the full-halfcode maximum is unique only within its stated multiset.

These are complete classifications only for the stated one-copy populations.
They do not exclude order-22 multisets with repeated codes and omitted types.
The enumeration corrects the evidence wording by distinguishing a unique
code subset, three labelled A-graphs, and one symmetry class.

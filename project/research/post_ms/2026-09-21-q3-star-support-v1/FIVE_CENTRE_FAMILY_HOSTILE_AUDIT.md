# Hostile audit of the five-centre family package

21 September 2026.  Independent same-slot replay; external review remains
open.

## Defects found and repaired

1. The first simultaneous-parity note stated the odd-`u` gap as `6u+18`.
   Direct substitution showed this was false.  The corrected balanced gap is
   `(11u+37)/2`; the construction, D2C proof and edge formula were unaffected.
2. “Unique minimum model” needed a sharper evidence label.  Exact enumeration
   finds three labelled order-21 A-graphs, related by coordinate permutations,
   hence one symmetry class.  At order 22, the full-halfcode population has a
   unique labelled 83-edge maximum, but duplicating S7 while omitting P0 gives
   a second 83-edge population mechanism.  All public claims now state their
   population scope.

  The family-envelope crossover is exact: ties at 22 and 26, linear family

  X3 remains installed as the mandatory negative control.

No further internal contradiction was found.

## Remaining trust boundary

The package proves explicit construction families and their internal density
maxima.  It does not classify arbitrary A-graphs on the positive five-centre
support, prove that the other two support orbits are impossible at arbitrary
multiplicity, or produce a positive maximum-root rigid cut.  Those three
limitations dominate the next work; none may be hidden by the large finite
replay counts.

# Independent graph-level regression through the Hall / pair-capacity interface

Date: 2026-09-19

Status: actual-graph audit package. This is regression evidence, not a substitute for the hand theorems and not a graph-realizability proof for abstract parameter states.

## 1. Why this package exists

The 19 September daily red-team audit correctly identified a shared upstream risk: several strong later inequalities had been rechecked algebraically, but the full map from an actual D2C graph into the rooted tight-code, selected-witness, Hall and pair-capacity variables had not been independently reconstructed in one place.

The source-premise repair package closed the two named finite-source-tuple premises and independently rebuilt the selected rooted B-edge / Hall ledger. The next mandatory step was therefore to continue the reconstruction through:

- tight A/U codes;
- direct and non-direct A-edge criticality;
- matched-foot gamma codes;
- A/U complementary witness localization;
- pair-local `L_P,S_P,Z_P,R_P,g_P,h_P`;
- exact `Ccap_P`;
- the exact Hall-cut decomposition;
- rigid singleton-head quantities whenever the hypotheses occur.

The checker in this package does that without importing the earlier source-premise checker.

## 2. Independent reconstruction

`check_rigid_graph_level.py` starts from a graph and rejects it unless it is actually diameter-2-critical.

For a maximum-degree root `v` it independently reconstructs:

1. `B=N(v)`, `A=V\N[v]`;
2. tight antipode pairs and unmatched set `U`;
3. Boolean codes of every vertex in `A union U` by its selected endpoint in each tight fibre;
4. one rooted B-edge criticality slot for every edge of `G[B]`, with injective source/A-label slot use;
5. unused slot counts `r_z`;
6. every A-edge as either direct (no common neighbour) or non-direct, with one explicit criticality certificate;
7. matched-foot gamma codes directly from the physical B adjacency;
8. the channel of every chosen non-direct certificate:
   - matched B-foot, checking `c(source)=gamma(w)`; or
   - A/U witness, checking complementary code;
9. complementary-pair local resources and traffic;
10. the exact code-radius pair capacity `Ccap_P`;
11. the exact Hall-cut identity

   `w_X-x(x-T0)=2E_X+M_X+J_X`;

12. the rigid singleton-head and U-deficit assertions if an actual cut satisfies `x>=3`, `M_X=E_X=0`.

Three deterministic certificate policies are used:

- lexicographic;
- matched-B first;
- A/U first.

This matters because the structural theorems permit an arbitrary fixed certificate choice. A transcription error that is hidden under one channel preference can therefore be exposed by another.

## 3. Actual-graph corpus

The run recorded in `RIGID_GRAPH_REGRESSION_SUMMARY.json` contains four components.

### 3.1 Entire graph atlas through order seven

- 21 unlabeled D2C classes;
- 50 maximum-degree roots.

### 3.2 Deterministically generated D2C corpus

For each order `8,...,14`, 100 graphs are generated by starting from `K_n` and deleting edges in a seeded random order whenever the resulting graph still has diameter at most two. The final graph is then rechecked edge-by-edge for D2C status before any rooted quantity is accepted.

This gives:

- 700 actual D2C graph instances;
- 1,127 maximum-degree roots.

The generation method is deliberately simple. It is not a uniform sampler and no statistical inference is made from frequencies.

### 3.3 Two fixed larger fixtures

`GdJ@^O` (order 8, size 13, root 7) has two occupied complementary A-pairs and exercises the exact Hall-cut decomposition.

`INPCw[`gW` (order 10, size 19, root 6) contains a non-direct A-edge with both an A/U certificate and a genuine matched-B certificate. Under the matched-first policy the regression therefore exercises the matched-foot gamma channel rather than merely reconstructing dormant `g_P` data.

### 3.4 Mandatory and extended cube-face controls

`X_3` is checked in full at its canonical cube root. The larger `X_4,X_5` cube-face graphs are separately checked for actual D2C status and exact rooted witness-slot saturation; they lie outside the tight-fibre branch (`p=0`) and are used as hostile controls for overinterpreting `r=0`.

Across all three certificate policies, the run contains `3,540` root-policy instances. The pair/slack package is applicable (`p>=1`, `lambda>=0`) on `114` of them.

The reconstructed checks are:

- 114 exact rooted residual identity checks;
- 255 local rooted-slot/direct-Hamming checks;
- 147 pair-local `Ccap_P` checks;
- 96 one-sided occupied pair checks;
- 75 A-edge certificates in qualified root-policy instances:
  - 27 direct;
  - 48 non-direct;
- one deliberately exercised matched-B A-edge certificate;
- 36 exact complementary-pair Hall-cut decompositions;
- zero mismatches.

The one-sided pair checks verify directly on actual graphs that, whenever only one of `c,bar c` is occupied in A, the two-sided matched-foot term satisfies

> `h_P=0`,

and the matched traffic obeys

> `P_P<=g_P max(n_c,n_bar c)`.

These are precisely the graph-level ingredients used in the companion one-code purification theorem.

## 6. A useful new hostile-control family

The companion note `CUBE_FACE_SATURATION_FAMILY.md` proves that the cube-face construction extends to an infinite D2C family `X_k`, `k>=3`, with

`n=2^k+k+1`,

`m=(k+1)2^k`,

and exact rooted slot saturation `r=f=delta=0`.

The regression reconstructs:

- `X_3`: `n=12,m=32,M=31,p=4,u=0,Q=12`;
- `X_4`: `n=21,m=80,M=101,p=0,u=16,Q=32`;
- `X_5`: `n=38,m=192,M=343,p=0,u=32,Q=80`.

So exact saturation is scalable, but the dangerous combination of saturation, tight-antipode transversality and above-`M(n)` density is dimension-three specific inside this family.

## 7. Coverage gap: no realized rigid cut found

This audit found **no actual rigid complete Hall cut** with `x>=3` in the bounded corpus.

That statement was not accepted after a single failed search. In addition to the 700 generated graphs, two targeted searches were performed:

1. 5,000 random D2C-preserving false-twin duplication walks starting from the small D2C atlas and fixed fixtures;
2. a further generated search on orders 8 through 17, 300 seeds per order, testing 64 roots that entered the nonnegative-lambda tight-fibre branch for a complete complementary-pair A-cut.

Neither produced a rigid cut.

This is a **coverage limitation, not evidence that rigid cuts are impossible**. It means the rigid singleton-head block is implemented in the checker but currently has no positive actual-graph fixture on which to execute.

Accordingly the companion one-code theorem must be read as a hand structural deduction from upstream ingredients that *are* graph-regressed, not as a small-graph empirical phenomenon.

That is the principal remaining audit risk at this interface.

## 8. What the regression changes in confidence

The graph-to-constraint chain is materially stronger than at the start of the audit:

- raw D2C -> rooted partition: independently reconstructed;
- rooted triangle edges -> injective B--A slots: independently reconstructed;
- A-edge direct/non-direct split: independently reconstructed;
- non-direct matched/AU channel localization: independently reconstructed;
- pair-local resource variables and `Ccap_P`: independently reconstructed;
- exact Hall cuts: independently reconstructed on actual multi-pair roots;
- one-sided-pair `h_P=0`: independently exercised;
- matched-B A-edge channel: at least one genuine actual-graph positive control exercised;
- `X_3`: still passes.

What has **not** been graph-exercised is the conjunction `M_X=E_X=0`, `x>=3`. The algebra and singleton-head proof should therefore retain a conditional trust label until either:

- an actual D2C fixture realizing the hypotheses is found; or
- an independent direct proof review of the rigid implication is completed at publication standard.

There is no graph/formula mismatch requiring a rollback.

## 9. Forward implication

With the audit boundary stated explicitly, the exact pair-local one-code line can now be sharpened without pretending the bounded graph corpus proves its realizability.

The companion theorem package finds two structural improvements that were invisible in the generic `(ONE)` statement:

1. a one-code outside pair has `h_P=0` exactly;
2. each outside source has at most `g_P` matched witnesses, giving the sharper U-witness floor `k_P=(x-g_P)_+`.

These yield the scorecard tradeoff

`S>=phi(g_P)+(x-g_P)_+(p-1)`

in the `p-y>=1` branch, and therefore the eliminated form

`S>=Psi_p(x)`.

The next work should preserve the local variables `g_P,u_bar,E_bar,L_Y,S_P` while combining the purified exact pair bill and the rooted residual ledger. A coarse collapse to total `C0` was explicitly tested and remains too lossy.

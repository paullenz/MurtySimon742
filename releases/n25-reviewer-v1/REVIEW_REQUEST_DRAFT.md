# Review request — draft, not sent

Subject: Request for an initial review of an order-25 Murty–Simon candidate

Dear colleague,

I am seeking an independent assessment of a candidate proof of the Murty–Simon conjecture at order 25: at most 156 edges in a diameter-2-critical graph, with equality only for K₁₂,₁₃.

The argument combines published reductions with residual-edge counting lemmas and a finite integer verification. A reviewer edition includes the manuscript, source code, complete compressed evidence, explicit rejection certificates and reproduction instructions. The computations have been cross-checked, but the argument has not received independent mathematical review.

I should disclose that the mathematical development, code and internal checking involved substantial ChatGPT/Codex assistance. The two arithmetic implementations were produced with the same assistant. I am presenting this as a candidate for scrutiny, not as an established result.

Would you be willing to take an initial look at the structural lemmas, particularly Sections 3, 5 and 6, or suggest a suitable reviewer? An identified gap or a correction to the literature context would be very helpful. I can send the manuscript and the frozen evidence package if you are interested.

Thank you for considering it.

Paul Lenz
